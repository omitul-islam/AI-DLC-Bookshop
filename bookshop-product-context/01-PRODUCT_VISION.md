# Product Vision

## 🎯 Product Vision

### Product Overview
The **Bookshop Management System** is a comprehensive solution designed to streamline bookshop operations including inventory management, customer records, and order fulfillment.

### Purpose
To provide bookshop owners and staff with a simple, efficient system to:
- Manage book inventory accurately
- Track customer information
- Process and fulfill orders seamlessly
- Maintain accurate stock levels

## 🏢 Target Users

### Primary Users
1. **Bookshop Owners** - Oversee operations, monitor inventory, and business metrics
2. **Store Managers** - Manage daily operations, inventory updates, and staff coordination
3. **Cashiers/Sales Staff** - Process customer orders and manage transactions
4. **Inventory Staff** - Update and maintain book records

## 🎨 Design Principles

### 1. **Simplicity First**
- Intuitive user interface
- Minimal learning curve
- Clear workflows

### 2. **Data Integrity**
- Accurate stock tracking
- Prevent overselling
- Consistent order status management

### 3. **Operational Efficiency**
- Fast book searches
- Quick order processing
- Real-time inventory updates

### 4. **Scalability**
- Support single or multiple store locations
- Handle growing inventory
- Accommodate increasing order volume

## 🎯 Core Features

### Book Management
- Add, update, delete books
- Search functionality (title, author)
- Track pricing and stock levels
- View complete inventory

### Customer Management
- Maintain customer records
- Track customer purchase history
- Quick customer lookup

### Order Management
- Create orders with automatic stock deduction
- Update order status (pending → shipped → delivered)
- Validate stock availability before order confirmation
- Order history tracking

### Inventory Control
- Real-time stock tracking
- Automatic stock reduction on orders
- Low stock alerts
- Prevent negative inventory
